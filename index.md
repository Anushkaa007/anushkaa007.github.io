## About

I am a neuroscience researcher working on systems and computational approaches to memory. My current work investigates how network hyperexcitability disrupts hippocampal-cortical oscillatory coordination and memory consolidation in a mouse model of Alzheimer's disease. I trained in life sciences and moved into neurophysics during my Master's.
