My overarching interests lie in understanding how the brain achieves memory, and how impairments lead to cognitive dysfunction and to the abnormal patterns of neuronal network activity that accompany several neurodegenerative disorders at the systems level. I am also inclined towards research on multisensory integration, predictive coding, and the dynamic interaction between sensory cortices and memory systems.

Investigating how intrinsic network hyperexcitability disrupts sleep-dependent hippocampal–cortical coordination in an Alzheimer’s disease mouse model, probing circuit mechanisms of memory destabilisation.
