I am a computational and systems neuroscientist at the Donders Institute, Radboud University, studying how aberrant network activity and circuit hyperexcitability shape memory consolidation, and how disruptions in these dynamics may be a subclinical signature of Alzheimer's disease. I also work on neural population dynamics with the Neural Data Lab at the University of Nottingham.

My Master's thesis at JNCASR, with K. S. Narayan, combined quantitative imaging, electrophysiology and computational modeling to ask how electroactive substrate geometry shapes neuronal network formation.
