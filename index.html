Computational Scientist & Quantitative Biology Researcher (M.S.) specializing in mathematical modeling, Python/MATLAB algorithm development, high-dimensional time-series analysis, and machine learning. Proven track record of building end-to-end data pipelines, simulating complex dynamical systems, and publishing peer-reviewed research (ACS Applied Bio Materials, Experimental Eye Research). Experienced technical peer reviewer (PLOS Computational Biology) and STEM mentor skilled in mathematical verification, scientific code optimization, and rigorous analytical evaluation.

I am currently working at the Donders Institute for Brain, Cognition and Behaviour, Radboud University, Netherlands on how aberrant network activity and circuit hyperexcitability shape memory consolidation, and how disruptions in these neural dynamics can be a subclinical signature of AD. My Masters' thesis at JNCASR under the supervision of Prof. KS Narayan, was to understand how electroactive substrate geometries affect growth, synaptic connectivity and neuronal network formation in-vitro, combining quantitative imaging, electrophysiology, and computational modeling.
